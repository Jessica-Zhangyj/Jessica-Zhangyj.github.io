🌏Life生活

I've always believed a life is measured in experiences. A car accident in my sophomore year — luckily not a serious one — taught me how suddenly everything can change. Whatever has happened, good or bad, has already happened; what deserves our energy is the present, and the road ahead. I hold expectations for the future, but I refuse to let them become a weight. Accept what comes, then keep walking. 我一直相信，人生的意义在于体验。大二那年的一场车祸——所幸并无大碍——让我明白一切都可能在一瞬间改变。无论好坏，过去的都已经过去，值得用力的是当下与前方。我对未来怀有期待，但不让期待成为负担；接受一切的发生，然后继续前行。

So my dream is simple: to see the world. I fly to Hong Kong and Macau for concerts, and lose myself in music festivals — wherever the music is, that's where the joy is. 所以我的理想很简单：去看世界。我喜欢飞去香港澳门看演唱会，喜欢在音乐节里尽情享受——音乐在哪里，快乐就在哪里。
